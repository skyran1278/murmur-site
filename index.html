What happens in those 23 seconds

  1. 01 You press once Action Button, Lock Screen, Control Center, Watch, or the app. The recording starts before the screen has finished waking up.
  2. 02 Audio hits the disk Written to storage first, every time — before transcription, before sync. A failed network never costs you an idea.
  3. 03 Your iPhone reads it back Speech recognition runs on the device. No cloud transcription service is involved, in any language.
  4. 04 It files itself A new page in your own Notion database, or a note in your own Obsidian vault, with the audio attached. You did not open either one.
